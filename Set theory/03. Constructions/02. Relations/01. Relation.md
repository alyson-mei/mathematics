- $R \subseteq X \times Y$ 
- $xRy := (x, y) \in R$
- $R \circ Q \subseteq X \times Z$ for $R \subseteq X \times Y$, $Q \subseteq Y \times Z$: $$R \circ Q := \{(x, z) \in X \times Z \mid \exists y \ (x R y \wedge y R z) \}$$
- $\mathrm{dom} \ R = \{ u \in X \mid \exists v \ u R v\}$,  $\mathrm{range} \ R = \{ v \in Y \mid \exists v \ u R v\}$
- $R[U] := \mathrm{range}(R \cup U\times Y)$
- $R^{-1} = \{ (y, x) \mid x R y \}$
